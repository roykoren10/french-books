# Petites histoires

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, generated with a dependency-free Node build. Hosting: GitHub Pages in the french-books repository.

## Users

French learners reading at CEFR A2, B1, or B2. Assumption delegated by the user: an English-speaking audience, often on a phone, reading for enjoyment and practice.

## Product Purpose

An inviting collection of original short French stories with optional English translations for each paragraph. Success means finding a suitable story and comfortably finishing it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fifteen A2, twenty-five B1, and twenty-five B2 stories (65 total).
- Different themes, characters, and settings; a mixture of humour, intrigue, culture, and gentle drama. Avoid very sad stories.
- A CEFR level picker, thumbnails for every story, paragraph translation toggles, and switchable dark/light themes.
- Short reading sessions: five paragraphs per story, approximately one or two pages.
- Public GitHub Pages publication explicitly requested.
- User explicitly delegated decisions and requested no questions.

## Product Principles

- Reading comes before interface decoration.
- Translations are a choice at paragraph level.
- Difficulty labels describe editorial targets, not a formal language assessment.
- Browser-local saved stories and completion state require no account.

## Evidence on Hand

Original story manuscripts in content/a2.json, content/b1.json, and content/b2.json, written by the requested GPT-6.1 Sol subagents. No invented reviews, user counts, or learning outcomes.

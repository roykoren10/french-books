Made for these stories

Every story cover is an original AI-generated gouache-style illustration, made for this collection with OpenAI’s image generation tool. The illustrations are imagined scenes, not photographs of real people or places.

Each cover has its own scene, drawn from its story. The covers are delivered in image atlases to keep the library light.

The typeface is Switzer by Indian Type Foundry, available from Fontshare, self-hosted under its free font license.

French stories and their English translations are original works created for Petites histoires. CEFR labels are editorial targets, not independently certified assessments.

Le premier grand bain

The First Big Swim

A2Everyday life4 min read249 words

An adult beginner discovers that learning to swim starts with a very small step.

À trente ans, Karim ne sait pas nager. Il aime la mer, mais il reste toujours sur le sable. Cette année, il a décidé de prendre des cours à la piscine municipale. Le premier samedi, il arrive très tôt. Dans son sac, il a une serviette et un bonnet neuf.

At thirty, Karim cannot swim. He likes the sea, but he always stays on the sand. This year, he has decided to take lessons at the town pool. On the first Saturday, he arrives very early. In his bag, he has a towel and a new swimming cap.

La professeure, Anne, lui montre le petit bassin. Ici, ses pieds touchent le fond et l'eau arrive à sa taille. Karim respire mieux. Avec trois autres adultes, il marche dans l'eau. Puis Anne leur demande de souffler sur la surface. Karim fait de petites vagues avec sa bouche.

Show English translationHide English translation

The teacher, Anne, shows him the small pool. Here, his feet touch the bottom and the water reaches his waist. Karim breathes more easily. With three other adults, he walks in the water. Then Anne asks them to blow across the surface. Karim makes little waves with his mouth.

Après cela, chacun prend une planche. Karim la tient devant lui et essaie de bouger les jambes. Il avance un peu, puis il s'arrête. Son bonnet est tombé sur ses yeux. Il ne voit plus rien ! Anne l'aide à le remettre. Les autres attendent et lui sourient gentiment.

After that, everyone takes a kickboard. Karim holds it in front of him and tries moving his legs. He moves forward a little, then stops. His cap has slipped over his eyes. He cannot see anything! Anne helps him put it back. The others wait and smile kindly at him.

À la fin du cours, Anne propose un dernier exercice. Karim garde les bras ouverts et pose le dos sur l'eau. La professeure le soutient doucement. Il regarde le plafond bleu. Pendant quelques secondes, il se sent léger. Il n'a plus envie de retourner tout de suite au bord.

At the end of the lesson, Anne suggests one last exercise. Karim keeps his arms open and lies back on the water. The teacher gently supports him. He looks at the blue ceiling. For a few seconds, he feels light. He no longer wants to return to the side straight away.

Dans le vestiaire, Karim écrit à sa sœur : « Aujourd'hui, j'ai flotté ! » Elle répond avec un grand soleil. Il range sa serviette humide avec plaisir. Il ne sait pas encore traverser la piscine, mais il va revenir samedi prochain. Avant de sortir, il achète un bonnet à la bonne taille.

Show English translationHide English translation

In the changing room, Karim writes to his sister: “Today, I floated!” She replies with a big sun. He happily packs his damp towel. He cannot swim across the pool yet, but he is going to return next Saturday. Before leaving, he buys a cap in the right size.

Et voilà.

## Initial material findings (now resolved)

Six affected stories; ten exact field replacements. Each French correction requiring a changed English meaning has a matching English correction, except the legal chess phrasing, whose existing English already expresses the intended flank move.

### 1. `a2-bonjour-de-toute-la-famille`, paragraph 5, fr

> la première vidéo avec la lampe

Replace with: **la deuxième vidéo, celle avec la lampe**

The first take shows half the grandmother’s face (paragraph 2); the ceiling/lamp is the second take (paragraph 3).

### 2. `a2-bonjour-de-toute-la-famille`, paragraph 5, en

> the first video with the lamp

Replace with: **the second video, the one with the lamp**

Match the corrected take number in French.

### 3. `b1-la-recolte-en-retard`, paragraph 1, fr

> le dernier arbre près du mur

Replace with: **un arbre près du mur**

The last tree implies the harvest is nearly complete, but paragraph 4 still has whole rows to harvest. An unspecified tree preserves the reunion and timetable.

### 4. `b1-la-recolte-en-retard`, paragraph 1, en

> the last tree beside the wall

Replace with: **a tree beside the wall**

Match the corrected harvest sequence in French.

### 5. `b2-montre-en-avance`, paragraph 1, fr

> sa montre avançait de sept minutes

Replace with: **sa montre retardait de sept minutes**

A watch perceived as seven minutes fast would make the client early. He is seven minutes late relative to the theatre clock, so he would suspect his own watch is slow.

### 6. `b2-montre-en-avance`, paragraph 1, en

> his watch ran seven minutes fast

Replace with: **his watch ran seven minutes slow**

Match the corrected direction of the apparent time error.

### 7. `b2-gambit-de-linvite`, paragraph 4, fr

> Il avança un pion de côté

Replace with: **Il avança un pion sur une aile**

A pawn cannot move sideways. A pawn advance on a flank is legal and matches the intended English meaning.

### 8. `b2-passage-du-chat`, paragraph 4, en

> on the first paving slab at children's eye level

Replace with: **on the first paving slab, where children could easily see it**

À hauteur des enfants is loose here, but the English makes it literally eye-level; the paving slab is on the ground. Preserve visibility without inventing a raised slab.

### 9. `b2-robe-des-deux-rives`, paragraph 3, fr

> elle n'avait nulle part où ranger le sien

Replace with: **ses poches étaient trop petites pour y ranger le sien**

Paragraph 2 gives the narrator’s design discreet pockets. The notebook should fail to fit those pockets rather than the dress suddenly having nowhere to store anything.

### 10. `b2-robe-des-deux-rives`, paragraph 3, en

> she had nowhere to put her own

Replace with: **her pockets were too small to hold her own**

Match the corrected pocket continuity in French.

The watch story ID/title can remain: the title can refer to the theatre clock being ahead. In paragraph 1, the narrator’s phrase “lui donnai raison sur un point” is slightly awkward because the client first alleges a faulty watch; “confirmai un point” would be optional polishing, not a required repair.

## Initial minor suggestions (now applied)

- `a2-le-cerf-volant-jaune`, paragraph 5, fr: “Ils prennent chacun leur tour jusqu'à l'heure du goûter.” → “Ils font voler le cerf-volant chacun à leur tour jusqu'à l'heure du goûter.”. More idiomatic explicit French for taking turns; keeps the A2 syntax transparent.
- `a2-le-tresor-de-la-place-bleue`, paragraph 2, en: “They note the last number” → “They note the last digit”. Chiffre is digit, while the complete date contains several digits. This makes the clue procedure precise.
- `b2-lot-quarante-deux`, paragraph 4, fr: “Lorsque le montant l’atteignit, elle baissa sa pancarte.” → “Lorsque sa dernière enchère atteignit cette limite, elle baissa sa pancarte.”. Clarify that Farida herself made the final bid; otherwise lowering her paddle can read as withdrawing before winning.
- `b2-lot-quarante-deux`, paragraph 4, en: “When the amount reached it, she lowered her paddle.” → “When her final bid reached that limit, she lowered her paddle.”. Match the clarification that the winning bid belongs to Farida.

Additional optional narrative clarification: `b2-souffle-du-studio`, paragraphs 2–3, contrasts wall-clock time with elapsed recording time, but does not explicitly explain why repeated test starts stayed synchronized with the compressor’s five-minute cycle. Add a short explanation if revising substantively; no automatic replacement is proposed. `b1-le-robot-de-minuit`, paragraph 4, could describe the old program as pushing dark objects toward light rather than following them, to explain its observed shoe delivery more directly. Neither undermines the gentle fictional premise.
